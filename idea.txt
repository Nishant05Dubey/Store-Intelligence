What we have built here is an AI-powered Store Intelligence System for physical retail environments (like a Purplle beauty store).

The Core Problem: The "Physical Retail Blindspot"
If you run an e-commerce website, you have tools like Google Analytics. You know exactly how many people visited the site, what pages they looked at, how long they stayed, and exactly where they abandoned their shopping cart.

However, in physical retail, store managers are almost completely blind. They only have data from their POS (Point of Sale) billing machines. They know what was sold, but they don't know:

How many people walked into the store but walked out without buying anything?
Which product aisles did customers spend the most time in?
Did we lose a sale because the billing queue was too long and the customer gave up?
What We Built to Solve It
We built a system that turns standard, "dumb" CCTV security cameras into a real-time analytics engine—bridging the gap between online analytics and offline physical stores.

The AI Pipeline (What you see in the video feeds): We used YOLOv8 and tracking algorithms to watch the CCTV footage. It identifies people, tracks their movement across the store, and uses advanced AI to distinguish between Customers (green boxes) and Staff (blue boxes).
Zone & Event Mapping: We mapped the camera pixels to physical store zones (like "Skincare", "Makeup", or the "Billing Queue"). The AI emits structured data every time a customer enters a zone, dwells there, or joins a line.
The Data Fusion Dashboard: We combined this CCTV movement data with the actual store Billing/POS data to give a complete view of the store's health in real-time.
The Business Value & Use Cases
From a business perspective, this dashboard allows a store manager to make data-driven decisions that directly increase revenue:

True Conversion Rate: By dividing the number of POS transactions by the total number of people who walked through the door, you get the True Conversion Rate. If 500 people enter but only 50 buy, a manager immediately knows there is a problem (perhaps poor staff engagement or missing stock) and can take action.
Queue Abandonment Prevention: The AI detects how many people are waiting in the billing queue. If the queue gets too long (a "Queue Spike" anomaly), it automatically alerts the manager to open a new billing counter before customers get frustrated and abandon their purchases.
Staff Optimization (Dead Zones): The heatmap tracks where customers are spending their time. If the system detects a "Dead Zone" (e.g., nobody has visited the Haircare section in 30 minutes), the manager can send floor staff over there to actively engage customers or realize that the product display needs to be moved to a higher-traffic area.
In short: We took existing security cameras and turned them into a tool that acts like Google Analytics for the real world, actively helping the business save lost sales and optimize staff placement.
